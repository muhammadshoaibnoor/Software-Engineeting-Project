\section{Introduction}
\subsection{Purpose}
This document is the Software Requirements Specification (SRS) for the Point-of-Sale (POS) System for a pizza shop chain. The purpose of this document is to describe the problem statement, the project objectives, and the stakeholder identification for the portion of the project assigned to the author (Allah Ditta). This SRS follows the Requirement Engineering principles taught in the course material and is written in a clear, testable manner to support later SRS extension and system development.

\subsection{Scope}
The scope of the assigned deliverable is limited to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Problem Statement
    \item Project Objectives
    \item Stakeholder Identification
\end{itemize}
These sections will be integrated into the complete SRS for the full POS system by the project team. The complete system will ultimately provide multi-branch support, dynamic menu management, inventory and cooked item handling, deal recommendations, order processing, reporting, and administrative controls.

\section{Problem Statement}
Many pizza shops that operate multiple branches face difficulties in managing menus, orders, inventory, and consolidated reporting. Typically, each branch maintains a different menu tailored to local customer preferences. This decentralization often results in:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Inconsistent menus and prices across branches.
    \item Difficulty in maintaining and updating branch-specific menus from a central location.
    \item Errors and delays in sales recording and report generation.
    \item Poor visibility for administrators over branch-level sales and inventory.
    \item Inefficient order management leading to preparation delays and customer dissatisfaction.
    \item Lack of a unified platform for customers to find and use the best available deals automatically.
\end{itemize}

Therefore, a centralized and smart POS system is required that connects branches, allows branch-specific menus, manages both inventory and cooked items, recommends applicable deals, and produces consolidated reports to support business decision-making.

\section{Stakeholder Identification}
The stakeholders listed below are identified according to the project scope and the Requirement Engineering slides. For each stakeholder we provide the role, responsibilities, and interests.

\begin{longtable}{|p{3.2cm}|p{3.6cm}|p{5.0cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Stakeholder} & \textbf{Role / Title} & \textbf{Responsibilities and Interests} \\
\hline
Requirement Provider & Client / End-user Representative & Provides requirements, validates functionality, reviews and signs off SRS, and provides feedback during development. \\
\hline
Administrator & System Owner / Head Office & Manages branches, monitors consolidated sales and inventory, creates and updates deals, configures branch policies, and reviews reports. \\
